This page describes what your team finds in the chat: history, search, model cards,
memories and the behavior of very long conversations. At the end there is a section for
administrators with the switches behind all of it.

The chat interface comes from [LibreChat](https://www.librechat.ai/docs). What we add is the
curation of the model cards, the stored system prompt and the permission handling on top.

<Aside type="tip" title="Four terms up front">

| Term | In one sentence |
|---|---|
| **Token** | The unit language models compute in: a piece of text of roughly four characters, so about half a word to a whole word. Everything you write and everything the colleague answers is counted in tokens, and usage, budgets and context limits all hang on that. |
| **Model card** | A ready made package of provider, model and presets that you pick under a comprehensible name instead of naming a raw model. |
| **System prompt** | The working instruction handed to the model ahead of your question, which sets language, tone and behavior. It belongs to the card and is not visible in the chat. |
| **Context** | Everything the model can read at once for one request: your question, the history so far, attached files and tool results. The room for it is limited and is measured in tokens. |

</Aside>

## Everyday use

You type a question and the answer appears progressively, word by word. Every conversation
lands in the history in the left column and is given a title automatically, derived by a model
from the first messages. You can overwrite that title at any time.

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| History | All conversations stay stored until you delete them or the retention period takes effect |
| Automatic titles | The title is created on the first exchange, without you doing anything |
| Bookmarks | Mark important conversations and find them again faster |
| Parallel answers | Run several chats side by side and compare the answers |
| Private chat | A conversation that is not stored |
| Fork | Open a new branch from any message without changing the original |
| Share | Publish a conversation as a link |

**"Privater Chat"** (private chat) is called **"Temporäre Chats"** (temporary chats) in the role
editor of the admin panel, where it carries the note "Unterhaltungen ohne Speicherung"
(conversations without storage). How long such a conversation is kept at all is set by the
admin.

**Forking** is the right move when a conversation should continue in two directions. You keep
the history so far and try a different variant in the branch, without losing the original
conversation.

**Sharing** creates a link to a snapshot of the conversation. Whoever has the link can read
along. The permission for this is a separate role permission and can be withdrawn.

## Searching old chats

The sidebar offers a search across the full text of all your own conversations. So the search
also finds a phrase in the middle of an answer from three weeks ago.

<Aside type="note" title="Three searches that are not the same thing">
The full text search across your chats is something different from **file search** across a
document collection, and different again from **web search**. Which one is responsible for
what is covered in
[Files and knowledge](/docs/en/funktionen/dateien-und-wissen) and in
[Web search](/docs/en/funktionen/web-suche).
</Aside>

## What a model card is

A model card is a ready made preset. It bundles three things:

1. a specific model at a specific provider,
2. the stored system prompt, meaning the working instruction given to the model,
3. the preselection of tools that are armed on this card.

The system prompt is inserted server side and never reaches the browser. The shipped cards
carry the same German prompt: answer in German, factual tone, no invented facts, ask back when
a request is unclear.

New chats start on the default card. You are allowed to switch cards, the switch is not
locked.

## Which cards you find

Which cards appear in the picker is decided by the admin. Two distinctions matter in daily
work:

- **Text, or text and images.** Not every card understands images. Screenshots and photos
  belong on a card marked as image capable; on a text only card an image has no effect.
- **EU or non EU.** Cards whose requests leave the EU carry the marking ⚠ Nicht-EU (non EU) in
  their name.

<Aside type="caution" title="The marking is deliberate">
The sign ⚠ and the addition "Nicht-EU" (non EU) are part of the card name so that it is visible
at the moment of choosing that this request leaves the EU. The marking must not be removed as
long as the card points at a provider outside the EU. Anyone who wants a purely European
instance does not offer such cards in the first place.
</Aside>

## Memories: the long term memory

The AI colleague remembers a few things about you across conversations and pulls them into
every new answer. This happens by itself, you do not have to say "remember this".

Deliberately little is stored, and only in four categories:

| Category | Content |
|---|---|
| Preferences | How you want answers: language, format, level of detail, tone, recurring corrections |
| Work context | Role, team, responsibilities, systems in use |
| Projects | Ongoing projects, customers and topics with a short status |
| Notes | Other stable facts that make future answers better |

Explicitly **not** stored are passwords and keys, health, religious and political information,
salary data, sensitive facts about third parties and one off details with no reuse value.

The store is deliberately kept small. When it fills up, the colleague merges related entries
instead of appending indefinitely.

**How to turn it off.** In the settings under **"Datenschutz & Sicherheit"** (privacy and
security), section **"Erinnerungen"** (memories), you find the switch **"Gespeicherte
Erinnerungen verwenden"** (use stored memories). Off means your memories no longer flow into
the answers. In the same area you can view your entries, edit them individually and delete
them.

## Very long conversations

A model can only read a limited amount of text per request. When a conversation gets very
long, a separate run summarizes the older messages automatically and from then on sends that
summary to the model instead of the full history.

- The point at which this kicks in depends on the card you chose: cards with a smaller context
  window compact earlier.
- Old tool results are cleared out first, for example long hit lists from a business system.
  Only after that do actual messages get their turn.

Your visible history stays complete throughout. Only what goes to the model is summarized.

<Aside type="note" title="There is no manual command for this">
A command such as `/compact` with which you trigger compaction yourself does not exist. The
process runs automatically and only automatically. If you want a deliberate fresh start, open a
new chat or fork at the appropriate point.
</Aside>

## For admins

### Model cards per role and group

Which cards a person sees is decided by the admin panel. The cascade is **group before role
before base**.

- **"Verwaltung → Rollen"** (administration, roles): one switch per card, per role. Plus the
  switch **"Weitere Modelle (Roh-Untermenü)"** (further models, raw submenu) with the note "Aus
  = diese Rolle sieht NUR die oben aktivierten Karten" (off means this role sees ONLY the cards
  enabled above).
- **"Verwaltung → Gruppen"** (administration, groups): per group either **"Erben"** (inherit) or
  **"Eigene Auswahl (übersteuert die Rollen-Regel)"** (own selection, overrides the role rule).

This is the clean way to grant management additional cards, for example, and show everyone else
only the EU cards.

### Hiding the model picker entirely

For a deliberately simple setup, set **"Modell-Auswahl anzeigen"** (show model selection) to
off in the panel under **"Konfiguration → Funktionen"** (configuration, features tab). Then
everyone works on the default card and no longer sees any model selection.

### Parameter panel

The panel for temperature and similar fine settings is **off** as shipped. That is deliberate:
team members without an AI background would otherwise change values whose effect they cannot
judge. Turning it back on is done per role through the configuration cascade in the panel; in
practice the role ADMIN gets that override and all other roles inherit the disabled behavior.

### A sensible order when setting up

<Steps>

1. Maintain prices and context sizes under **"Konfiguration → Preise"** (configuration, prices)
   so that usage metering is correct. An empty price field does not mean free, LibreChat then
   calculates with a built in flat rate.

2. Define the cards per role and group.

3. Only then invite your team.

</Steps>
